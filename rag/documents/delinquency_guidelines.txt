Delinquency Guidelines - Synthetic Project Knowledge

Payment delinquency is an important credit-risk signal.

The project derives a delinquency score using weighted payment events:

- 30-59 days past due: weight 1
- 60-89 days past due: weight 2
- 90+ days past due: weight 3
- 120+ days past due: weight 4

More severe or repeated delinquency events can indicate increased repayment risk.

Late payment count is also considered relative to credit history duration.

The model should be interpreted as a probabilistic risk estimate, not a guaranteed prediction.
