Credit Risk Guidelines - Synthetic Project Knowledge

Credit utilization:
Credit utilization represents the proportion of available revolving credit currently being used. Higher utilization can indicate increased dependence on available credit.

Recent credit inquiries:
Multiple recent inquiries may indicate active credit seeking. This should be considered together with income, debt, payment history, and other credit indicators.

Credit history:
A longer, established credit history can provide more behavioral information. Credit history length should not be interpreted independently from repayment behavior.

Bankruptcy and tax liens:
Previous bankruptcies and tax liens may be relevant adverse indicators. They require context and should not be treated as automatic rejection criteria.
