Loan Policy - Synthetic Project Knowledge

Purpose:
This document provides synthetic policy guidance for the financial risk demonstration system. It is not a real lending policy and must not be used for real credit decisions.

General principles:
- Evaluate repayment capacity before considering loan approval.
- Consider income, debt burden, credit utilization, delinquency history, recent inquiries, and previous adverse credit events together.
- A single metric should not be treated as a guaranteed indicator of default.
- Higher observed risk should lead to additional review rather than an automatic lending decision.

Risk interpretation:
- Low risk: applicant indicators generally support repayment capacity and show limited recent adverse behavior.
- Medium risk: one or more indicators require additional review.
- High risk: multiple indicators suggest elevated repayment or credit-management risk.

Recommended action:
Use the model result as a decision-support signal. Perform additional verification and human review before any real lending decision.
